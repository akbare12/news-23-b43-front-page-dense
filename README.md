A one-image front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026's world news:
the AI rout that hit Asian markets (Kospi −3.3%), the UN chief's call to
regulate AI before it's too late (Bridgetown summit), and the US lead over
China falling to a record low (Asia Power Index 2026, gap 5.3) — plus 28 more
stories across 6 regions, a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK
box separating what happened on 23 November from what did not.

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.png` (1024×1024) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. `draw.py` (Pillow) renders the page.
